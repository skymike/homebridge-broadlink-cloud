BroadLink Cloud

Connect your RM MAX remotes to Apple Home. This version supports EU cloud accounts.

1. Connect your home

Sign in or reconnect

Credentials stay on your Homebridge. Saving the account stores them in its settings for automatic re-login. Sign-in alone does not replace your saved session.

2. Choose a remote

A momentary button sends one command and resets. It does not report the appliance's physical state.

The selected remote matches the verified TCL profile. Its RM hub should be in the same room as the AC for temperature and humidity readings.

3. Review and save

Restart Homebridge after saving. Saving mappings does not operate appliances. Removing a mapping disables its cached accessory.
